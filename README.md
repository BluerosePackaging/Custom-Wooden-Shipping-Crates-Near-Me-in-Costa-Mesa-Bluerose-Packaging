## How to Choose the Right Wooden Shipping Crate

Selecting the correct crate requires more than measuring the product. Consider the complete shipment and how the cargo will be handled from packing through final delivery.

- **Measure the product:** Record its length, width, height, and any protruding sections.
- **Confirm the weight:** Provide the total product weight so the crate structure can be planned appropriately.
- **Review product sensitivity:** Identify delicate surfaces, exposed components, or areas that need additional cushioning.
- **Select the crate style:** Decide whether an open, closed, or partially enclosed design is appropriate.
- **Plan lifting and handling:** Share forklift entry points, lifting arrangements, and loading requirements.
- **Consider transportation:** Road, air, and sea shipments may involve different handling and environmental conditions.
- **Review export requirements:** For international shipments, confirm applicable wood packaging regulations and treatment requirements for the destination.
- **Discuss internal protection:** Determine whether foam, blocking, wrapping, or other protective materials are needed.

## Wooden Crates for Shipping, Storage, and Export
